02 / CULTURE

Culture is more
than a tradition.

Culture is a shared way of life. It includes what people create, believe, value, practice, and pass from one generation to another.

Choose a culture lens.

Tap a button below. The information panel changes with your choice.

Material culture

Physical objects made, used, or valued by a group of people.

Filipino examples: bahay kubo, jeepneys, barong Tagalog, woven baskets, and traditional instruments.
03 / SOCIETY

We shape society.
Society shapes us.

Society is not just a place. It is a network of relationships, shared rules, groups, and institutions. Tap each topic to see how it works.

Social groupsPeople who interact, share connections, or feel a sense of belonging. Examples include families, friend groups, classmates, and community organizations.
04 / POLITICS

Who decides?
Who participates?

Politics involves power and decision-making in groups and communities. Tap each colorful block to explore a key idea.

Power

The ability to influence decisions, actions, or the behavior of others. Power can exist in governments, schools, families, workplaces, and communities.
